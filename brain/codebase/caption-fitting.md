# Fitting captions to the drawn size

- `fit_captions()` wraps captions where phwstyle draws a chart at a known size: `phw_save()`, `phw_finalise()` (in `makeContext`), `phw_add_slide()` and the knitr render hook. Plain `print()` and `ggsave()` still get the fixed 70-character wrap of `wrap_caption()`.
- **patchwork flattens captions into its own gtable.** Plot k's caption is the cell `caption-k`, the figure caption is `caption`. The last plot of a patchwork is the patchwork object itself; `patches$plots` holds the others. A grob patch has a `zeroGrob` caption cell.
- **Resolve a gtable's layout with grid, not by hand.** Push `viewport(layout = grid.layout(nrow, ncol, widths, heights, respect))`, then a child viewport with `layout.pos.row/col`, and read `deviceLoc()`. This handles null units, respect matrices and the centring of fixed-shape charts.
- **Right-aligned captions under side-by-side fixed-shape panels may have no fit.** The right caption can only be as wide as the gap between the two panels' right edges, and each extra line makes the map shorter and so narrower. A 2:1 tall map in 16 x 10 cm has no wrapping that fits, and looping until it fits collapses to one word per line. Keep the pass that runs over least. A pass whose captions leave a panel no room (negative width or height) counts as running over without end: its measured overflow is meaningless and once looked like a fit (#376).
- **In a patchwork the fallback is to gather the plots' captions under the figure** (`gather_captions()`), each text once, when no pass fits or when the room the captions take pushes a plot's title into the one beside it. A map beside a chart, or two maps in a finalised figure at 16 x 10 cm, has no wrapping that fits under the map. Two maps without finalise still fit side by side and keep their own captions.
- **Testing drawn text positions:** `rvg::dml_pptx()` works as a `phw_save(device =)` function and writes each drawn line as a `p:sp` with `a:off` x and `a:ext` cx in EMU (914400 per inch). Set `raster_prefix` to a temp folder or colour bars land in the working directory. Its font metrics differ slightly from ragg's.
- **Titles of a patchwork's plots wrap last.** A map beside a chart can leave its title less room than its text (the right edge of the figure or the next title in the row). `fit_captions_here()` wraps captions first, then gathers them, and only if text still runs over runs the passes again with titles and subtitles (`caption_slots(plot, all_text)`). Wrapping titles together with captions from the start made a narrow figure (8 x 9 cm) collapse on pass 2, so the unwrapped pass 1 was kept and the caption ran off the left edge, worse than before (#419).
- **A fixed-shape panel's title widens the plot instead of wrapping narrow (#434).** A map beside another plot is sized by the figure's height, so every line its title gains shrinks the map and the title's room with it: wrapping spiralled to one word per line and a thumbnail map whenever a figure title, the `phw_finalise()` footer, a half-slide size or a tall collected legend took height. `refit_captions()` now wraps such a title only to an equal share of the row (`(end - start) / titles in row - gap`) and returns `widen`, the inches still missing, which `widen_right()` adds to that plot's right margin. The flexible plot beside it, or a centred figure's spare width, absorbs it. Fixed shape comes from the plot (`coord$is_free()`, `aspect.ratio`), not the gtable: stacked plots share columns, so a respected column does not say which plot is the map.
- **Titles side by side need a visible gap.** A title's room to the next title is less one M at its own size; without it a fit put "Rate by health board Weekly cases" a space apart, reading as one title.
- **A label a slot sets keeps the text as given** (`phw_unwrapped`), so the titles pass wraps the gathered caption from its source text, not line by line from an earlier wrap ("Licence / v3.0. Contains OS / data").
- **A chart beside a widened map can be too narrow for its axis labels.** That is ggplot drawing category labels in half a figure: `phw_arrange(b, b)` overlaps the same way. The equal share keeps the chart at least that wide.
